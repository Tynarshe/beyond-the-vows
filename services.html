EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

The little details.

Where are you based?

We’re based in the East Midlands and are happy to travel across the UK and beyond to capture your special day.

How will we receive our content?

Raw footage is delivered within 24 hours through an online gallery link. Edited highlight Reels are delivered within 72 hours. Download and save your raw gallery promptly before the time-limited link expires.

Are travel and accommodation included?

Travel costs are covered by the client and calculated separately. Accommodation may be required for longer journeys or where coverage begins early.

How many content creators will be there?

Each package includes one content creator. If a second shooter is required, this can be arranged and priced upon request.

How do we secure our date?

Once we’ve confirmed availability and prepared your quote, a 50% deposit and signed service agreement are required to secure your wedding date. The remaining balance is due four weeks before your event.
